About Sports Equity Systems

Sports Equity Systems (SES) is designed to create financial equity in baseball by aligning payroll with team revenue. Instead of allowing large‑market teams to outspend everyone else, SES ties spending power directly to what each team earns. This creates a balanced, predictable financial environment where every team can compete on equal footing.

How SES Improves Financial Equity

  • Teams spend based on revenue, not market size
  • Payroll becomes predictable and sustainable
  • Player compensation aligns with performance and contribution
  • Small‑market teams gain competitive stability
  • The league becomes more balanced overall
